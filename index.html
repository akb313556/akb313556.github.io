About

Your business operations partner

Brennan Consulting Services was established in 2020 by Ashley K. Brennan, Owner and Principal. Based in Calgary, Alberta, BCS works remotely with organizations across Canada and the United States as an independent operations partner.

Ashley is the person who keeps the business running: operations, finance administration, payroll, HR, compliance, vendors, insurance and projects. She has supported leaders, including serving as a fractional Chief of Staff and as Director of Operations across multiple businesses under common ownership.

With more than 15 years of executive-level experience, she works in English and French and brings the same standard to every engagement: prompt, precise and proficient.

Ashley K. Brennan

Founder & Principal

Ashley is a dedicated mother and entrepreneur. She founded Brennan Consulting Services to provide for her daughter while keeping the flexibility to be present in her daughter’s everyday life. She serves as a fractional COO, Chief of Staff and business operations partner, and prides herself on being there for her clients at all times and building close, trusted relationships with them.

“Throughout my 15-plus years of working business relationships, I understand how important trust is. As business professionals, we deal with a lot of confidential information. We want to build that trust with our clients and help them succeed personally and professionally.”

With more than 15 years supporting executives and owners, Ashley has served as a fractional Chief of Staff and as Director of Operations across multiple businesses under common ownership. She runs operations end to end, including bookkeeping, payroll, HR, compliance, insurance and vendor management, as well as fleet and vehicle purchase decisions. She makes decisions under the limits owners set, with regular check-ins.

When she isn’t behind her computer, Ashley is often travelling around North America supporting her daughter as she races at motocross events. Her educational background includes Criminal Justice, Legal Assistant studies, and Business Administration with a focus in Accounting.

How we work

We take decisions off your plate

The goal is simple: fewer things on your desk, and confidence that everything else is handled properly.

  1. Learn how you decide

    We start by understanding your priorities, your standards and how you make decisions.

  2. Take ownership

    Then we take those decisions and tasks off your plate, acting within the authority you set.

  3. Bring solutions

    When problems come up, we bring them to you with options and a recommended solution.

  4. Document everything

    Every process, decision and record is documented, so nothing depends on memory.
