About

Your business operations partner

Brennan Consulting Services was established in 2020 by Ashley K. Brennan, Owner and Principal. Based in Calgary, Alberta, BCS works remotely with organizations across Canada and the United States as an independent operations partner.

Ashley is the person who keeps the business running: operations, finance administration, payroll, HR, compliance, vendors, insurance and projects. She has supported leaders at large organizations, including serving as fractional Chief of Staff to the President of a large real estate sales and marketing firm, and making operational decisions for a multi-site RV resort and development company.

With more than 15 years of executive-level experience, she works in English and French and brings the same standard to every engagement: prompt, precise and proficient.

How I work

I take decisions off your plate

The goal is simple: fewer things on your desk, and confidence that everything else is handled properly.

  1. Learn how you decide

    I start by understanding your priorities, your standards and how you make decisions.

  2. Take ownership

    Then I take those decisions and tasks off your plate, acting within the authority you set.

  3. Bring solutions

    When problems come up, I bring them to you with options and a recommended solution.

  4. Document everything

    Every process, decision and record is documented, so nothing depends on memory.
